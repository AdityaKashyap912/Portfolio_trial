Landing Gear Dynamics: designing a main-gear strut for off-design landings

A 1-DOF model of an oleo-pneumatic main gear (nonlinear gas spring, quadratic orifice damping, hard stop). Three designs were compared at the design landing, then stress-tested with off-design scenarios and a 120-case Monte Carlo study over sink speed, lift, mass and gas charge.

Findings

  • A metering pin and a softer gas spring both reduce peak load, but the softer spring spends stroke margin.
  • Design C has the lowest load at the design point, yet it bottoms out in about 1 in 5 random landings. Design B is the robust choice.
  • Judging designs at a single operating point can select the wrong design.

Limitations

  • Parameters are illustrative and not validated against drop-test data.
  • One degree of freedom: no tyre or wheel mass, constant lift, no friction or thermal effects.
  • Rebound is simplified, so the model rebounds at about 2 m/s.
